## Understanding Rudra (रुद्र)

**Rudra in Vedic tradition**:
- The Howler, The Roarer (rudra = "to cry, howl")
- Storm god with power over lightning and tempests
- Fierce destroyer who transforms through destruction
- Healer who destroys disease
- Often depicted with:
  - Matted hair (jata) flowing like storm clouds
  - Trishula (trident) weapon
  - Blue/dark complexion (like storm clouds)
  - Fierce expression, third eye
  - Tigers, serpents, or storms surrounding him

**For RUDRA framework**: The storm that destroys bugs to transform code

## Mythological Accuracy Notes

**Traditional Rudra iconography**:
- **Hair**: Matted locks (jata), often piled high, wild
- **Skin**: Dark blue or ash-grey (like storm clouds)
- **Eyes**: Fierce, often with third eye visible
- **Weapons**: Trishula (trident), bow and arrow, sometimes drum
- **Posture**: Standing in powerful stance or dancing (tandava)
- **Garments**: Tiger skin, serpents as ornaments, minimal clothing
- **Companions**: Tigers, serpents, sometimes Nandi (bull)

**RUDRA framework interpretation**:
- Storm = Chaos testing
- Trishula = Three-pronged approach (chaos + eBPF + VMs)
- Third eye = Seeing hidden bugs
- Howl/Roar = Tests that shake code to its core
- Destruction = Destroying bugs
- Healing = Making code robust

## Usage Guidelines

**Respectful representation**:
- Rudra is a revered deity - treat with respect
- Focus on storm/power/transformation aspects
- Avoid trivializing or mocking
- Emphasize fierce-but-protective nature

**Technical balance**:
- Blend mythology with modern tech aesthetics
- Don't make it too "fantasy" - keep professional
- Storm and chaos imagery should feel powerful, not cartoonish
- Suitable for developer tool branding

**Color consistency**:
- Stick to blue/purple/electric palette
- Dark backgrounds (dark mode friendly)
- High contrast for visibility
